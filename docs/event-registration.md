# Event registration

Sky and TvT NPC windows display registration status, participants, remaining registration time, entry requirements and rewards supplied by the server. Closed registrations cannot be selected. The gold frame fits the panel and prize lines wrap at spaces. Vulkan supports the tall Paris interface artwork without changing world texture limits.

Generals, Guild Battle and Horse Race use the same approved Paris windows as the classic client. Clan events show registered clans separately from players. Entry requirements, mount requirements, duration, laps and rewards come from the server. Reward arrows browse two configured rewards per page. Closed events remain unavailable, and the server validates every registration.

TvT has a compact black/gold scoreboard above the lower right action bar. Drag its title to move it; use the minus/plus control to minimize or restore it. Scores, team and remaining time come from the server. Notices use the Events color category. Team transformations and balanced health, mana, speed and damage are configured in the server TvT editor. Normal attributes are restored when the event ends or the participant leaves.

TvT team transformations clear companion visuals on entry. Equipment appearance updates also remove stale companions before rebuilding real equipped pets, including after leaving or cancelling the event. Reconnecting is not required to clear a companion left by the previous appearance.
